PATTERN THIS SETS FOR THE OTHER SEVEN
  • One hero panel per screen, bordered in its verdict ink (red / amber / lime), carrying the single figure that answers the screen. Everything else is ledger rows.
  • Refusal never empties a screen: inputs stay, derived figures go to —, and the checklist becomes the content.
  • Magenta appears exactly once per screen, on the gap panel — shortfall or headroom, always signed and always worded.
  • Underline badges throughout; solid red slug is the only filled badge in the app, reserved for stale.
  • Manual and user-supplied values: amber left edge on rows, dashed underline on badges.

Two calls I made that you may want to overrule: the hero is EV rather than P(10) needed — EV is the decision, but P(10) is the more transferable figure across Signals and Comparison. And in 2c the headroom panel uses magenta for a positive gap, which spends the app's one gap ink on good news as well as bad.

FLAGS · WHERE A DESIGN WOULD ASSUME MORE THAN THE SCHEMA GUARANTEES
  • card_ref.image_url is nullable and null in every fixture. The "art breathes" idea in 1b needs a designed no-art state that is not a grey box — I would use the foil dither plus set code as the art surrogate.
  • grade_ladder has minItems: 1 and no requirement that all four grades appear, so the ladder must render with 1–4 rungs, not assume four.
  • price_history has no guaranteed density or ordering and the fixture holds two points per grade — a real chart may be two dots. The overlay design must read as two dots without looking broken.
  • refusal.missing is a bare string array with no titles, per-item reason codes, or deep links, so a nine-item checklist can name items but cannot route to them unless the strings match assumption ids. Worth pinning down before I build the refusal state.
  • No screen_manual_entry exists in the schema, and no field marks a row as manually entered except buy_route.entry_method. The "manual rows look different everywhere" rule has no carrier on ladder rungs or signal rows today.
  • Staleness is per-screen and per-row, one kind at a time — a row mixing a fresh price with stale population cannot express both at once.
  • needs_primary_verification lives only on grading_tier_view in Settings, so "provisional" cannot be derived on the Grading Lab figure it affects; today only the envelope warnings string carries it.
  • Trend Radar is the eighth screen in the schema; the brief's eight list Manual Entry instead. I will design nine surfaces unless you'd rather drop one.
