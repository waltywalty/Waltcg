BLOCKERS FOR THE PROTOTYPE
  • Per-figure staleness (4d, bottom panel) has no carrier: staleness is one kind per row, so a fresh-pop / stale-price row can't be expressed.
  • Empty (4b) counts rows that matched but failed friction. Nothing returns rejected candidates — without it, empty degrades to a bare line.
  • Error (4c) needs a cache timestamp distinct from as_of: "as of 11:04" is not the same claim as "fetched at 11:04".
  • Track Record's median needs a minimum-n floor (I used 30) before it shows at all — a policy decision, not a design one.

3 Eight screens · locked pattern

Cabinet Ledger, both rulings applied. Magenta stays the one gap ink on either sign. And the hero rule now has two forms: measured inputs → the hero is the verdict figure (EV, margin, hit rate); user-supplied input drives it → the hero is the claim pair, needed vs yours, with the money demoted to a secondary row and marked as resting on your number. That holds on Grading Lab 3d2, Manual Entry 3e, and any Signals row whose P(10) you typed. Trend Radar is out; Manual Entry is the eighth. Figures express relationships, not audited arithmetic — ladder widths, gap signs and hierarchy survive a fixture correction.

STILL OUTRUNNING THE SCHEMA
  • Nothing marks a signal row's P(10) as user-supplied, so the 3b needed/yours row and the 3g worst-call provenance both need the manual-entry marker you're having added to reach signals, not just prices.
  • Track Record has no fixture. Hit rate, excess return, per-play breakdown and the worst-five ordering are all invented shapes — they need a schema before 3g is real.
  • The friction stack in 3f assumes fee bands are expressible as ordered tiers with boundaries. If the backend stores a flat effective rate, the expansion has nothing structural to show.
  • Assumption entries in 3h show a "used by N figures" count — a reverse dependency the registry doesn't currently expose.
